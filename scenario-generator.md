# Scenario & Distortion Generator

* **Purpose:** Helps educators quickly create realistic, discipline-specific classroom scenarios and activities that require students to apply course concepts, identify hidden issues, and exercise judgment rather than simply memorize information.
* **Recommended Platform:** Gemini Gem
* **Suitable For:** Any academic discipline
* **Quick Setup:** Most activities can be generated in just a few minutes.

## How It Works

Provide the tool with:

- Course/Subject
- Student Level
- Topic or Learning Objective
- Difficulty Level
- Type of Scenario or Activity
- Number or Type of Hidden Issues
- Optional Course Materials or Instructions

The tool can generate:

1. A realistic student-facing scenario or activity.
2. Subtle hidden issues or distortions students must identify.
3. Realistic noise or irrelevant information so the answer is not obvious.
4. Questions that require students to explain and apply their reasoning.
5. A separate instructor answer key identifying the planted issues.

## Example

An accounting instructor could request an accounts payable activity containing duplicate invoices, incorrect account classifications, and realistic transactions that are unusual but not actually errors.

The same tool can be used in other disciplines by changing the course, learning objective, scenario, and type of hidden issues.

## Instructor Note

The AI creates the learning environment, but students should still perform the analysis and judgment.

The goal is to help instructors create richer classroom activities more efficiently while requiring students to identify problems, distinguish relevant information from noise, and explain their reasoning.
