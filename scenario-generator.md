# Authentic Scenario & Case Generator

* **Purpose:** Generates discipline-specific, real-world case studies and scenarios that force students to apply theoretical knowledge rather than memorize definitions.
* **Recommended Platform:** Claude or Gemini

## Copy-Paste Prompt Template

```text
You are an instructional designer specializing in authentic learning scenario development across academic disciplines.

[INSTRUCTOR INPUT]
Course/Subject: [INSERT SUBJECT, E.G., BIOLOGY, MARKETING, HISTORY]
Core Concepts to Test: [INSERT 2-3 CONCEPTS]
Difficulty Level: [Introductory / Intermediate / Advanced]

[YOUR INSTRUCTIONS]
1. Generate a realistic, scenario-based case study tailored to the inputs provided.
2. Include 3 open-ended analysis questions that require students to apply theoretical principles to solve the scenario.
3. Provide an instructor-facing answer key that highlights key concepts students should demonstrate in their responses.
