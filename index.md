# AI Teaching Toolkit

Welcome to the **AI Teaching Toolkit**—a practical, discipline-neutral resource designed to help educators integrate interactive AI tools into their courses.

Rather than relying on static essay assessments or generic prompts, these tools focus on cognitive process evaluation, Socratic guidance, and robust skill-building.
